## GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY

### Phase 1: Launch & Validation (Months 1-3)

**Objective:** Get first 5 paying customers, validate product-market fit

**Key Tactics:**

1. **Direct Outreach**
   - Identify 50 private SPT training programs
   - LinkedIn outreach to owners/directors
   - Personalized emails with demo video
   - Offer: 50% off first year for early adopters

2. **Content Marketing**
   - Publish "SpeedGrader vs Canvas" comparison
   - Write "How to Save 10 Hours/Week Grading" guide
   - Share on LinkedIn, industry forums

3. **Partnerships**
   - Contact IAHCSMM and CBSPD (certification organizations)
   - Propose: "Preferred LMS Partner" status
   - Offer: 10% revenue share or co-marketing

4. **Demo Strategy**
   - Create 5-minute product demo video
   - Offer live 1-on-1 demos (30 min)
   - Give 2-week free trial with sample content

**Success Criteria:**
- 5 paying customers
- 10 testimonials/reviews
- <30 day sales cycle
- >80% trial-to-paid conversion

---

### Phase 2: Scale & Expand (Months 4-12)

**Objective:** Reach 25 customers, $250K ARR

**Key Tactics:**

1. **Case Studies** - Film 2-3 customer success stories showing time saved and student outcomes
2. **Paid Advertising** - Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads targeting SPT program directors ($2K-5K/month budget)
3. **Conference Presence** - Exhibit at IAHCSMM and CBSPD conferences
4. **Referral Program** - Offer $500-1000 per referral or 1 month free
5. **SEO & Content** - Target "sterile processing LMS", publish 2 blog posts per week
6. **Community Building** - Facebook group for SPT educators, monthly webinars

**Success Criteria:**
- 25 total customers
- $250K ARR
- 500+ trial signups
- <60 day average sales cycle
- NPS > 50

---

### Phase 3: Enterprise & Scale (Months 13-24)

**Objective:** Land 3-5 enterprise contracts, reach $1M+ ARR

**Key Tactics:**

1. **Enterprise Sales Team** - Hire 1-2 sales reps, 1 customer success manager, implement CRM
2. **RFP Response Service** - Create template responses, highlight security and uptime
3. **Strategic Partnerships** - Partner with nursing schools, hospital associations, medical equipment vendors
4. **Product Expansion** - Add AR features (camera-based instrument ID), VR training modules, analytics dashboard
5. **International Expansion** - Canada, UK, Australia markets
6. **Content Marketplace** - Allow instructors to sell courses (20-30% commission)

**Success Criteria:**
- 3-5 enterprise customers (>$75K each)
- $1M+ ARR
- <90 day enterprise sales cycle
- 90%+ customer retention
- Net dollar retention >110%

## SALES PROCESS

### Tier 1 Customers (2-4 weeks)

**Week 1: Discovery**
- Initial outreach (LinkedIn/Email)
- 15-min discovery call to understand pain points, current process, budget
- Send demo video and case study

**Week 2: Demo & Trial**
- 30-min live product demo customized to their use case
- Offer 2-week free trial with sample content and training

**Week 3: Evaluation**
- Check in after 1 week of trial
- Answer questions, address concerns
- Show ROI calculator (time saved)

**Week 4: Close**
- Send proposal with pricing
- Negotiate terms
- Sign contract and onboard customer

---

### Tier 2 Customers (1-3 months)

**Month 1: Relationship Building**
- Multiple touchpoints with department head, IT, procurement
- Understand approval process, timeline, budget cycle
- Provide case studies from similar schools

**Month 2: Pilot Program**
- Run 90-day pilot with 1-2 classes
- Provide training, support, check-ins
- Collect usage data, feedback, testimonials

**Month 3: Expansion**
- Present pilot results to decision makers
- Show time saved, student outcomes, instructor satisfaction
- Propose full rollout with pricing
- Negotiate contract terms and sign multi-year agreement

---

### Tier 3 Customers (3-9 months)

**Months 1-2: Initial Contact & Discovery**
- Identify decision makers
- Formal RFP or needs assessment
- Multiple meetings with stakeholders
- IT security review and budget approval process

**Months 3-5: Proposal & Pilot**
- Submit detailed proposal
- Security audit and legal review
- Pilot program with limited users
- Collect metrics and feedback

**Months 6-9: Negotiation & Close**
- Present pilot results
- Negotiate pricing, SLA, support
- Legal contracting and procurement process
- Sign agreement and kickoff full implementation

## RISK MITIGATION

### Key Risks & Solutions

**Risk 1: Low Market Awareness**
- Mitigation: Heavy content marketing, partnerships with certification orgs, conference presence, testimonials

**Risk 2: Long Sales Cycles**
- Mitigation: Start with Tier 1 (faster cycles), offer free trials, build urgency with limited-time discounts

**Risk 3: Product Not Sticky**
- Mitigation: Track usage metrics closely, proactive customer success outreach, regular feature updates

**Risk 4: Price Objections**
- Mitigation: Show ROI calculator (time saved), offer payment plans, compare to expensive alternatives

**Risk 5: Technical Issues**
- Mitigation: 99.9% uptime SLA, 24/7 monitoring, quick bug fixes, transparent status page

**Risk 6: Competition from Canvas**
- Mitigation: Emphasize niche focus, show feature comparison, highlight SpeedGrader advantage, win on customer service
