**Primary LMS:** Canvas (Instructure)
- Used by 10 university divisions
- Supports academic and non-academic courses

**Secondary Platform:** CoursePlus
- Custom-built by Center for Teaching and Learning (CTL)

### Stanford Medicine
**Multi-Platform Approach:**

1. **Canvas** - Primary LMS for all Stanford courses
   - Managed by Educational Technology team
   - University-wide standard

2. **LearnMed** - Specialized Canvas instance
   - Managed by EdTech team
   - Delivers medical education content
   - For staff, students, and faculty
   - Extends to collaborating institutions worldwide

3. **Docebo** - "Stanford Learning Experience"
   - Provides flexibility for online learning
   - Supports live teaching/coaching via Zoom
   - Alternative to Canvas for specific use cases

4. **LearnDash** - Med Education (MedEd) platform
   - More versatility in course development
   - Better enrollment management
   - Enhanced course management features

**Key Insight:** Stanford uses Canvas as the foundation but layersadditional platforms for specialized medical education needs.
