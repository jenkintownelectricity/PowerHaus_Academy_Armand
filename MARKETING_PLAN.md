## 🚀 Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch & Validation (Months 1-3)

**Objective:** Get first 5 paying customers, validate product-market fit

**Tactics:**
1. **Direct Outreach**
   - Identify 50 private SPT training programs
   - LinkedIn outreach to owners/directors
   - Personalized emails with demo video
   - Offer: 50% off first year for early adopters

2. **Content Marketing**
   - Publish "SpeedGrader vs Canvas" comparison
   - Write "How to Save 10 Hours/Week Grading" guide
   - Create "7 LMS Features SPT Teachers Actually Use" blog
   - Share on LinkedIn, industry forums

3. **Partnerships**
   - Contact IAHCSMM (certification org)
   - Contact CBSPD (certification org)
   - Propose: "Preferred LMS Partner" status
   - Offer: 10% revenue share or co-marketing

4. **Demo Strategy**
   - Create 5-minute product demo video
   - Offer live 1-on-1 demos (30 min)
   - Give 2-week free trial with sample content
   - Follow up within 24 hours

**Success Criteria:**
- 5 paying customers
- 10 testimonials/reviews
- <30 day sales cycle
- >80% trial-to-paid conversion

### Phase 2: Scale & Expand (Months 4-12)

**Objective:** Reach 25 customers, $250K ARR

**Tactics:**
1. **Case Studies**
   - Film 2-3 customer success stories
   - Show: Before/after, time saved, student outcomes
   - Use in sales presentations
   - Feature on website homepage

2. **Paid Advertising**
   - Google Ads: "SPT Training Software", "LMS for Sterile Processing"
   - Facebook/LinkedIn Ads: Target SPT program directors
   - Budget: $2K-5K/month
   - Target CPA: $500-1000

3. **Conference Presence**
   - Exhibit at IAHCSMM conference
   - Speak at CBSPD conference
   - Host workshop: "Future of SPT Education"
   - Collect 100+ leads per conference

4. **Referral Program**
   - Offer: $500-1000 per referral
   - Or: 1 month free for each referral
   - Create referral portal for customers
   - Track with unique referral codes

5. **SEO & Content**
   - Target keywords: "sterile processing LMS", "SPT training software"
   - Publish 2 blog posts per week
   - Create ultimate guide to SPT certification
   - Build backlinks from medical edu sites

6. **Community Building**
   - Create Facebook group for SPT educators
   - Host monthly webinar: "Best Practices in SPT Education"
   - Build email list (offer: Free SPT quiz bank)
   - Engage in Reddit, nursing forums

**Success Criteria:**
- 25 total customers
- $250K ARR
- 500+ trial signups
- <60 day average sales cycle
- NPS > 50

### Phase 3: Enterprise & Scale (Months 13-24)

**Objective:** Land 3-5 enterprise contracts, reach $1M+ ARR

**Tactics:**
1. **Enterprise Sales Team**
   - Hire 1-2 dedicated sales reps

2. **RFP Response Service**

3. **Strategic Partnerships**
   - Partner with nursing schools
   - Partner with hospital associations
   - Partner with medical equipment vendors
   - Co-marketing opportunities

4. **Product Expansion**
   - Add AR features (camera-based instrument ID)
   - Add VR training modules

   - Add white-label option for large customers

5. **International Expansion**
   - Canada market (similar regulations)
   - UK market (NHS training needs)
   - Australia market
   - Translate to Spanish for LatAm

6. **Content Marketplace**
   - Allow instructors to sell courses

   - Opens additional revenue stream

**Success Criteria:**
- 3-5 enterprise customers (>$75K each)
- $1M+ ARR
- <90 day enterprise sales cycle
- 90%+ customer retention
- Net dollar retention >110%

## 🎬 Sales Process

### For Tier 1 Customers (2-4 weeks):

**Week 1: Discovery**
- Initial outreach (LinkedIn/Email)
- 15-min discovery call
- Understand: Pain points, current process, budget
- Send: Demo video, case study

**Week 2: Demo & Trial**
- 30-min live product demo
- Customize demo to their use case
- Offer: 2-week free trial
- Provide: Sample content, training

**Week 3: Evaluation**
- Check in after 1 week of trial
- Answer questions
- Address concerns
- Show: ROI calculator (time saved)

**Week 4: Close**
- Send proposal with pricing
- Negotiate terms
- Sign contract
- Onboard customer

### For Tier 2 Customers (1-3 months):

**Month 1: Relationship Building**
- Multiple touchpoints
- Meet department head, IT, procurement
- Understand: Approval process, timeline, budget cycle
- Provide: Case studies from similar schools

**Month 2: Pilot Program**
- Run 90-day pilot with 1-2 classes
- Provide: Training, support, check-ins
- Collect: Usage data, feedback, testimonials
- Prepare: Final presentation with results

**Month 3: Expansion**
- Present pilot results to decision makers
- Show: Time saved, student outcomes, instructor satisfaction
- Propose: Full rollout with pricing
- Negotiate: Contract terms, integrations
- Close: Sign multi-year agreement

### For Tier 3 Customers (3-9 months):

**Months 1-2: Initial Contact & Discovery**
- Identify decision makers
- Formal RFP or needs assessment
- Multiple meetings with stakeholders
- IT security review
- Budget approval process

**Months 3-5: Proposal & Pilot**
- Submit detailed proposal
- Security audit
- Legal review
- Pilot program with limited users
- Collect metrics and feedback

**Months 6-9: Negotiation & Close**
- Present pilot results
- Negotiate: Pricing, SLA, support
- Legal contracting
- Procurement process
- Sign agreement
- Kickoff full implementation
